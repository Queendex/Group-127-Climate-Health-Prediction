Group 127 – Climate Risk and Health Predictions

About the Project

Welcome to Group 127’s Climate Risk and Health Predictions project!

We are a team of  members working together on a Data Science and Machine Learning capstone project. Our goal is to explore how climate-related factors may be linked to health outcomes and use data to help predict potential health risks.

Project Overview

We will explore relevant climate and health data, look for patterns, and develop machine learning models to investigate and predict health risks associated with climate conditions.

Our work may include:

* Exploring and understanding the available data.
* Cleaning and preparing datasets for analysis.
* Studying relationships between climate factors and health outcomes.
* Building and evaluating machine learning models.
* Documenting our findings and limitations.

Team Collaboration

All members will contribute through smaller working groups, with each group taking responsibility for specific tasks and sharing progress with the wider team.

We will use GitHub to:

* Share and manage project files.
* Track changes to our work.
* Collaborate on code and documentation.
* Review contributions and improve our work together.

Project Structure

* data/ – Approved datasets and processed data.
* notebooks/ – Data exploration and experiments.
* src/ – Python scripts and model development.
* docs/ – Project documentation.

Our Goal

To investigate climate-related health risks responsibly, build useful data science skills, and learn and grow together as a team.
